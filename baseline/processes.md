## Ticketing Workflow

<!-- Describe your ticket lifecycle -->
1. **New** — ticket created (client email, phone, portal, or internal)
2. **Triaged** — categorized, prioritized, assigned
3. **In Progress** — technician working
4. **Waiting on Client** — awaiting info or approval
5. **Resolved** — fix applied, client notified
6. **Closed** — confirmed resolved, time entered

## Escalation Path

<!-- How issues escalate through your team -->
1. **Tier 1** — Service desk handles first contact, basic troubleshooting
2. **Tier 2** — Senior technicians for complex issues
3. **Tier 3** — Engineering / specialists for infrastructure, security, or architecture
4. **Management** — client-impacting outages or SLA breaches

## Client Onboarding

<!-- Steps when bringing on a new client -->
1. Sales handoff — signed agreement, scope, contacts
2. Discovery — network assessment, documentation audit
3. Agent deployment — RMM, security tools, backup
4. Documentation — build out in [documentation platform]
5. User setup — M365, MFA, security awareness training
6. Go-live — cutover support, introduce service desk
7. 30-day check-in — review, adjust, confirm satisfaction

## Change Management

<!-- How you handle changes to client environments -->
- **Standard changes** — pre-approved, low risk (e.g., user adds, password resets)
- **Normal changes** — require approval, scheduled maintenance window
- **Emergency changes** — break/fix, documented after the fact
