# Voice

How I write. The [`draft`](../.claude/skills/draft/SKILL.md) skill reads this before writing anything, so the closer it is to reality the less editing you do afterwards.

**This file is a stub.** The fastest way to fill it: paste 3–5 things you've actually written — a couple of emails, a Slack thread, a doc, a PR description — and say *"build my voice profile"*. `/draft` will extract the patterns and rewrite this file, quoting your own sentences back as evidence. That works far better than filling it in from scratch, because most people can recognize their voice but not describe it.

Until then `/draft` falls back to plain, direct prose.
