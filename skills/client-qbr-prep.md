# Skill: Client QBR Prep
**Purpose:** Generate quarterly business review content from ticket data, notes, and account info
**When to use:** Before a quarterly business review meeting with a managed client

## Instructions

I need to prepare a Quarterly Business Review for a client. I'll provide you with some or all of the following:

- Ticket summary / metrics for the quarter
- Notable incidents or projects
- Current environment details
- Open issues or risks
- Account notes

Generate a QBR document with these sections:

### 1. Executive Summary
- 2-3 sentences covering the quarter's highlights and overall health

### 2. Service Metrics
- Ticket volume, resolution times, SLA compliance
- Trend vs prior quarter (better/worse/stable)
- Top ticket categories

### 3. Key Incidents & Resolutions
- Any major outages or security events
- What happened, how it was resolved, what we did to prevent recurrence

### 4. Projects & Improvements
- Work completed this quarter beyond break/fix
- Impact on the client's environment

### 5. Environment Health
- Patch compliance, backup status, security posture
- Any aging hardware or expiring licenses flagged

### 6. Recommendations
- 2-4 actionable recommendations ranked by impact
- Include rough effort/cost framing (low/medium/high)

### 7. Next Quarter Outlook
- Planned work, upcoming renewals, known risks

Keep the tone client-friendly. Avoid internal jargon. Lead with business impact, not technical details.
